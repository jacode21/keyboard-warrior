Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use Keyboard Warrior at www.playkeyboardwarrior.com (the “Site”), operated by Jacob Slusser (“we”, “us”).

No accounts, no personal data we collect

The Site has no sign-up, login, or account. We do not ask for your email, and we don’t run our own analytics or sell data. The game runs entirely in your browser.

Data stored on your device (localStorage)

To remember your preferences and progress, the game stores small values in your browser’s localStorage: theme, sound setting, speed, shortcut pool, platform (Windows/Mac), optional player name, high scores, daily-challenge results and streak, local XP, rank and achievements, Workflow best times, and Keyboard Hero settings and scores. Short per-visit session goals are kept in sessionStorage and disappear when you close the tab. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser’s site data. If you type a player name, it is only included in a share or challenge link when you choose to share it.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent message managed through Google’s certified consent tools will ask for your choice before personalized ads or ad cookies are used. Non-personalized ads may still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.
