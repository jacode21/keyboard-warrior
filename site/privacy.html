The fine print

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use Keyboard Warrior at www.playkeyboardwarrior.com (the “Site”), operated by Jacob Slusser (“we”, “us”).

No accounts

The Site has no sign-up, login, or account. We do not ask for your email, and we don’t run our own analytics or sell data. The game runs entirely in your browser.

Data stored on your device (localStorage)

To remember your preferences and progress, the game stores small values in your browser’s localStorage: theme, sound setting, speed, shortcut pool, platform (Windows/Mac), optional player name, high scores, daily-challenge results and streak, local XP, rank and achievements, Back Office best times, and Keyboard Hero settings and scores. Short per-visit today's targets are kept in sessionStorage and disappear when you close the tab. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser’s site data. If you type a player name, it is only included in a share or challenge link when you choose to share it.

The Big Board (online leaderboard), opt-in

If you choose Post to the Big Board (or switch on automatic posting), we store on Cloudflare: the trader name you typed (max 16 characters, filtered), an optional desk code, your score and run stats (fill rate, best streak, mode, track or job, keyboard or touch), and the time. To limit abuse we keep a salted, truncated hash of your IP address, never the IP itself; rate-limit counters expire within two minutes and used run IDs within three days. Leaderboard rows are public on the Big Board. To remove a row, email us with the name and time.

Anonymous usage counts

The game sends anonymous event counts (for example "session started", "session finished", "share tapped", "duel started") that are added to a per-day tally. No identifiers, cookies or IP addresses are stored with these counts. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free, privacy-first page analytics service that does not track you across sites.

Duel links

A duel link contains your trader name, score, speed and a per-second score trace of your session, encoded in the URL itself. It is only shared when you send it.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The Site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (or google.com/settings/ads).
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps, and Google’s advertising policies and Privacy Policy.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent message managed through Google’s certified consent tools will ask for your choice before personalized ads or ad cookies are used. Non-personalized ads may still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

Hosting and logs

The Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the Site. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The Site is intended for a general and workplace audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can clear localStorage and cookies in your browser settings, use the ad opt-outs above, or use an ad blocker. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA; because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests relating to ads should be directed to Google via the links above, and anything else to us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
