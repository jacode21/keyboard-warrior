About

Keyboard Warrior is a free browser game that turns Excel keyboard shortcuts into a trading-floor arcade. Built for office workers who want to stop reaching for the mouse — in quick sessions between meetings.

The listings

  • Arcade: a 60-second session. Orders fall as Excel commands; key in the real shortcut to fill each one before it lands on the book. Streaks, jackpots, four tape speeds.
  • Daily Tape: the same orders for every trader each day, with challenge links to beat your desk.
  • Back Office: close out realistic spreadsheet jobs (P&L, trade blotter, comps) on a simulated sheet, with a live keystroke log, par paths and grades.
  • Keyboard Hero: a rhythm game on original, synthesized beats. Arrow keys for the lanes, F2 for the $ drop.

Works on Windows and Mac (real Excel for Mac shortcuts). On phones and tablets, a touch desk takes over: Quick Pick tickets, or a keypad that builds the combo. No accounts, no downloads; your P&L lives in your browser.
